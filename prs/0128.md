## Description

Follow-up to [#118](./0118.md). Under the XWayland DPI hack the prompt could render at
the previous scale factor's size — the bar around it correct, the dialog
un-counter-scaled — and, when it didn't, it still landed clipped off the
right screen edge on the first popup after every HudScaleChanged.

Both come from the same GTK behaviour: a widget's style is validated while
it is mapped and left alone otherwise, so the popover, hidden across the
scale change, keeps the style it was last shown under. It then measures —
and can paint — at that size, while the always-mapped bar restyles the
moment apply_scale reloads the stylesheet. Measured at factor 2.0 with the
prompt hidden across the change: the surviving label measures 218px against
a true 427px, and re-rooting the contents does not clear it; a label built
after the change measures 366px (183px at factor 1.0), i.e. current.

So build_confirm_prompt() now builds the whole prompt — popover, content
box, label, buttons, handlers — and the scale-change branch of the HUD
timer calls it again after apply_scale, popping down and unparenting the
old one. The widgets never survive a scale change, so they cannot carry a
stale style into a measurement or into a frame, and align_popover_to_button
can simply measure (the ratio-correction heuristic this replaces is gone).

Also in this pass:

- Box spacings (bar and prompt) and the TimeDisplay clock icon now follow
  the factor like the other icons and the sliders. They are widget
  properties, which scale_px_literals never reaches, so the counter-scaled
  HUD was consistently tighter than the same UI on a native-scale HiDPI
  panel — 25px short at factor 2.0, now 13, the rest being font leading.
- get_hud_scale (HidpiController::factor), seeded by the HUD on every
  connect. HudScaleChanged is broadcast once, at launch, so a shell that
  was not subscribed at that instant — started late, or dropped and
  reconnected mid-activity — had no way to learn the factor for the rest of
  the session. Same reason DisplayController::state exists.
- XwaylandHidpi::apply warns when every output is already at scale 1.0:
  normal on a 1x panel, but on a HiDPI one it means a previous session's
  restore was lost, and from a shell's side the two are indistinguishable.
- apply_scale logs the factor it applies; prompt alignment logs its inputs
  at debug. Enough to tell "never got the factor" from "got it and
  mis-laid-out" in a field report.
- SHEPHERD_HUD_DEBUG_CONFIRM_TRIGGER, a debug-build hook that pops and
  dismisses the prompt from a shell, is now permanent: the headless harness
  cannot click a GTK button, and three agents in a row have hand-added and
  removed a one-shot version of it.

Verified headlessly at output scales 1.5 and 2.0 against a two-entry
fixture differing only in xwayland_native_resolution: hacked and reference
prompts now land at the same position and size, the first popup after every
transition is correct (previously clipped entering the hack, ~196px adrift
leaving it at factor 2.0), and a screenshot burst shows the first painted
frame already correct. A HUD restarted mid-activity now seeds factor=1.5
and comes up counter-scaled instead of sitting at 1.0.
