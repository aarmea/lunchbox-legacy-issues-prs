## Description

Fixes [#68](https://github.com/aarmea/lunchbox/issues/68)

Introduce a per-activity `input_compat = "disable_touch"` mode that grabs
every touchscreen and discards its events, disabling the touchscreen for the
lifetime of an activity — for games that misbehave on touch but remain
playable with a mouse or gamepad.

Rather than add a new sidecar crate, reuse shepherd-touch-bridge: a new
`--grab-only` flag grabs the touchscreens (the existing EVIOCGRAB path) and
drains/discards their events instead of synthesizing pointer motion. No
virtual pointer is created, so this mode needs the `input` group but not
/dev/uinput.

touch_to_mouse, tablet_to_touch, and disable_touch all grab or produce the
touchscreen, so they are now mutually exclusive via a generalized
InputCompatMode::handles_touch() conflict guard; disable_touch still stacks
with a gamepad preset.
