## Description

An activity that crashes seconds after launch used to start its full
cooldown, locking the child out of something they never got to play. With
a group cooldown that took the whole category down with it.

A session shorter than the subject's cooldown_min_session (default two
minutes) now leaves the cooldown alone. Configured globally with
service.cooldown_min_session_seconds and per subject with
limits.cooldown_min_session_seconds; 0 restores the old behaviour of
cooling down after every session however short. Entry and group
thresholds are evaluated separately against the same session, so a
category can forgive a crash that the activity itself still cools down
for.

Only cooldowns are affected: usage accounting and token earn/spend still
count the seconds actually played.

The grace is dodgeable by design — quitting just under the threshold
never triggers a cooldown, and the daily quota is what still bounds that.
Documented as a gotcha next to the other group/token surprises.
